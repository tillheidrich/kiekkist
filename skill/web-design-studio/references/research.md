# Research

The model's memory of "good websites" is a small, outdated set of the same famous sites. Real research breaks that. It also gives the client something they trust more than adjectives: "here is what your competitors do, here is what the best in your field do, here is what we take from each".

## Rules

- If this session has a web search, a fetch tool or a browser, using it is mandatory. Recalling sites from memory is allowed only to generate search ideas, never as a reference.
- Every reference is a page you actually looked at in this session, with a screenshot (`kk_add_ref` captures desktop, mobile and full page).
- Every reference gets a **mechanism sentence**: what exactly you take, and why it works for this audience. Format: "<element> <does what>, which <effect on this audience>."
- Every reference also gets a **do-not-take** note when there is an obvious trap: "do not take the 3D hero; it carries no information".
- Collect **12 to 20**. Fewer than 12 and you are choosing from your habits. More than 20 and the board stops being readable.
- Record the date you viewed it. Pages change.

## The lanes

| # | Lane | How many | Where to look |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client's current presence | all relevant pages | their site, Google Business profile, social |
| 2 | Direct competitors | 3 to 5 | search "<service> <town>", Google Maps, directories, the client's own list |
| 3 | Best of the industry | 2 to 3 | national leaders, award databases, galleries filtered by industry |
| 4 | Neighbouring industries | 2 to 3 | same audience or same promise in another trade (see table below) |
| 5 | Gallery finds | 3 to 6 | `kk_list_sources` or `sources.md`, filtered by industry and type |
| 6 | Template marketplaces | 2 to 3 screens | ThemeForest, Webflow, Framer category pages |
| 7 | Beyond the web | 3 to 5 | print, editorial, signage, packaging, archives: `sources.md` → "Beyond the web"; one per concept card you plan to test |
| 8 | Anti-reference | exactly 1 | the look the client named, or the category's most generic template |

**Why beyond the web.** Web galleries show what other web designers already did, so references from them pull every direction towards the same few layouts. Print, signage, packaging and archives hold structures the web has not worn out yet (a timetable, a catalogue raisonné, a wayfinding system). They feed the concept cards in `concepts.md`. Take the logic of the form, not its period costume. Capture them like any reference (`kk_add_ref` with a mechanism); for scans in books or archives without a URL, photograph or describe them and note the source.

**Craft and local trades: mobile first.** For trades and local services, look at mobile references first (loadmo.re), then desktop galleries: the audience meets these sites on a phone.

**Why the marketplaces.** A template marketplace is the best index of a category's conventions: what thousands of buyers in that category accepted as normal. Use it to name the rut ("every estate agent template has a search bar over a drone photo"), not as a source to copy. The safe direction may use the convention; the bold direction should know it is leaving it.

**Neighbouring industries** carry mechanisms the client's competitors have not used yet:

| Client | Look at |
|---|---|
| Estate agent | architecture studios, boutique hotels, interior magazines, museum collection pages |
| Roofer, carpenter, craft trade | product manufacturers (tools, windows), outdoor equipment brands, timber construction firms |
| Law firm, tax adviser | publishers, university departments, think tanks, private banks |
| Hairdresser, beauty | independent fashion labels, perfume houses, specialty coffee roasters |
| Restaurant, hotel | wineries, farm shops, small publishers, local tourism boards |
| Industrial B2B | engineering consultancies, scientific instrument makers, infrastructure operators |
| Software tool | hardware manuals, instrument panels, transit maps, other tools with the same user |
| Medical practice | pharmacies with good information design, public health services, care providers |

## Blendwerk: name it

Mark a reference as Blendwerk when its effect depends on something the client cannot have or that does not help the audience:

- Paid 3D, WebGL or video that carries no information.
- A photo shoot budget far beyond the client's (a beauty brand's campaign images on a local salon brief).
- Template demo content: invented statistics, fake client logos, stock testimonials. The layout may be fine; the proof is fiction.
- Award-site showreels where navigation is hidden and content is thin.
- Dribbble shots that were never built and do not survive real text lengths.
- Sites that only look good at one viewport.

Keep a Blendwerk reference only if a mechanism survives without the trick, and write which one.

## Search terms

Combine an industry term with a quality term and, for competitors, a place. Use both languages; German results give you the real local market, English results the international bar.

**Quality and gallery terms**

| German | English |
|---|---|
| beste Websites <Branche> | best <industry> websites |
| Webdesign Beispiele <Branche> | <industry> website design examples |
| <Branche> Website Inspiration | <industry> website inspiration |
| Website Relaunch <Branche> Agentur Referenz | <industry> website redesign case study |
| <Branche> Webdesign Award | <industry> site of the day |
| Corporate Design <Branche> | <industry> brand identity case study |
| <Branche> Präsentation Design | <industry> pitch deck design |
| <Branche> Instagram Beiträge Design | <industry> instagram post design |
| <Branche> App Design | <industry> app UI |

**Competitor terms:** `<Leistung> <Ort>`, `<Leistung> in der Nähe von <Ort>`, `<Branche> <Landkreis>`, plus the client's own competitor list. Check Google Maps for the local field; the businesses with the most reviews are the ones customers compare against.

**Search operators that help:** `site:siteinspire.com <industry>`, `site:minimal.gallery <industry>`, `site:behance.net <Branche> Corporate Design`, `site:fontsinuse.com <industry>`, `intitle:"<industry>" template`.

## Worked example: "good websites for estate agents"

The request: a regional estate agency in northern Germany wants a new site. Research plan:

1. **Client presence:** current site, Google Business profile, the listing portals they use (their listings there show their real photos and copy).
2. **Competitors (4):** search `Immobilienmakler <Stadt>`, `Immobilie verkaufen <Stadt>`, `Immobilienbewertung <Landkreis>`. Pick the two biggest local brands and two independent agents.
3. **Best of the industry (3):** search `beste Makler Websites`, `real estate agency website design`, `luxury real estate website`; filter galleries: minimal.gallery tag "real estate" and "architecture interior design", Siteinspire subject filter, One Page Love search "real estate", Land-book category for real estate.
4. **Neighbouring (3):** an architecture studio's project index, a boutique hotel's room pages, an interior magazine's article layout. Mechanisms to look for: how a single property is presented as a story, how floor plans and facts sit next to photos.
5. **Galleries (4):** from the filters above, pick sites whose mechanism is new to the competitor field.
6. **Marketplaces (2 screens):** Webflow template category for real estate (`webflow.com/templates/category/real-estate-websites`, verified 29.10.2026), ThemeForest search "real estate". Name the rut you see there, for example: search bar over a full-width drone photo, three-up property cards with price badges, agent portrait grid, counter of "sold properties".
7. **Anti-reference (1):** the most generic franchise agency site in the region.

Example mechanism sentences:

- "The project index lists each building as one line with year, place and size, which lets a seller judge the agency's track record in five seconds."
- "Floor plan and facts sit in a fixed column beside the photo sequence, so the buyer never scrolls away from the numbers."
- Blendwerk: "Drone video loop in the hero: impressive, but it delays the first content on mobile and every competitor already has one. Take nothing."

## After research

Write the board entries (`kk_add_to_board` with the note) and go to Phase 3. If a lane stayed empty, say which and why (for example "no direct competitor has a website; lane 2 shows Google profiles only"). An empty lane is a finding, not a failure.
