# Sources

Fallback list when `kk_list_sources` is not available. Kiekkist keeps the same data in its `sources` table and may hold a newer version; prefer it.

**Status column.** "ok" means the site answered with its expected content from a plain HTTP request on 29.10.2026. "bot wall" means it answered with a bot check or rate limit to a script on that day; it exists and normally works in a real browser, so open it with a browser tool. Re-check anything older than a few months. Galleries change owners, paywalls and focus.

## Beyond the web (feeds concept cards)

These hold structures from print, editorial, signage, packaging and archives. Use them for the concept cards (`concepts.md`), not for page layouts. Status: not checked by script on 29.10.2026; open with a browser tool.

| Source | URL | Good for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fonts In Use | fontsinuse.com | Type in real print, signage, packaging and books; searchable by format and topic | Commercial faces dominate |
| It's Nice That | itsnicethat.com | Current graphic design, editorial, identity and illustration projects | Skews to studio showpieces |
| Are.na | are.na | Channels curated by designers: archives, signage, ephemera, diagrams | Unsorted; sources often missing |
| Standards Manual | standardsmanual.com | Reissued design manuals (transit, space agencies, rail): systems thinking | Shop; take the logic, not the retro look |

| ADC Deutschland | adc.de | German award work across media | Award bias |

The client's own world is the best source of all: forms, invoices, signs, vehicles, tools, packaging, certificates. Photograph or ask for them in the briefing.

## Do not use (maintainer's source reviews)

- **Sales funnels or off-topic:** getlayers.ai, oratory.co, createanything.com, app.webjourney.pro, peekinsights.co, boobook.world, noobpreneur.com, coda.io, friendventure.de (only its footer disclosure is worth a look)
- **Unchecked or bait posts:** jiro.build, Bklit UI, Manus, Doory, MotionClone
- **Asset libraries with the AI look:** unDraw, Humaaans, Heroicons, Lucide, Phosphor, Material Icons. Draw or photograph instead
- **Dribbble:** details only, never a page layout (see above)

## Template marketplaces as a category index

| Source | URL pattern | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| Webflow | `webflow.com/templates/category/<category>`, for example `real-estate-websites` (verified 29.10.2026) | Screenshot two or three of the top category results; name the conventions they share |
| ThemeForest | `themeforest.net/search/<term>` | Same; the sales count shows which conventions buyers accept (bot wall to scripts) |
| Framer | `framer.com/marketplace/templates/` and category pages | Same; Framer templates show the current startup aesthetic (rate limited to scripts) |

Do not capture template demo pages as positive references unless a specific mechanism is worth it; their content is fictional by design.
