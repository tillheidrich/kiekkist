# Code of conduct

Be kind and specific. Criticise work, not people. No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this project's spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block people who break this. Report problems to security@heidrich-digital.de; reports are handled confidentially.

This is a short version of the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
