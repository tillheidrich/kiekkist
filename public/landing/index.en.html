How a project runs

Low German: kieken means to look, Kist is a box.

Why AI designs all look the same

A language model reaches for the average of what it has seen. That is why almost every draft ends up with the same picture: a purple gradient, glass, three cards with icons, a small pill above every heading. A longer prompt often helps very little, because the model is not missing the wish, it is missing the material.

An agency works differently. It looks at real work, commits to a direction before showing the client, and remembers on the next project what was chosen. Kiekkist gives the model exactly these three things.

How a project runs

The skill kiekkist-agency takes Claude through the same steps a small agency follows. Every step leaves something behind that you can look at.

Memory
Claude reads your taste profile, earlier themes and the client's brand rules before it even thinks about colors.
Briefing
Questions in short rounds: offer, audience, promise, proof, tone, and what it must not turn into.
Research
12 to 20 real sites from the industry, from competitors and from neighboring fields. For each one, a sentence on what exactly works about it.
Moodboard
The finds are sorted by mechanism, not by color, with a scene: who looks at this page, and when?
Three directions
Safe, middle, bold. Three real HTML pages with the client's real content, differing on at least three of five axes.
Decision
You choose, or your client chooses through a link without a login. The reason and any wish to combine directions are recorded.
System and build-out
The chosen direction becomes DESIGN.md, tokens and every medium: pages, slides, social graphics, app screens.
Check and critique
First Kiekkist measures, then a second agent that has not seen the build gives its review. Two rounds of corrections at most.
Filing
The theme goes into the gallery, your verdict goes into the profile word for word. The next project starts smarter.

One theme, every medium

What you see in the draft is the result. Not a mockup that has to be rebuilt later, but HTML with self-hosted fonts and colors in OKLCH. Website, slide, social graphic and app screen come from the same tokens.

Example from a German small-business project: Nordlicht Immobilien, an invented estate agent in Hamburg's western suburbs along the Elbe. Photos: Till Heidrich.

Four companies, four attitudes

The example themes, all from German small-business projects, show how far apart the directions can be. Each one passes the check with grade A.

  • Kanzlei Brandt Weller

    A law firm, purely typographic. Margin numbers and case references instead of images.

  • Harms Präzisionstechnik

    A precision engineering shop. The page as a technical drawing, tolerances in µm.

  • Studio Elbe

    Premium consulting with a single thesis and a decision log.

  • Nordlicht Immobilien

    An estate agent. Magazine layout, large photos, the listing as a data sheet.

Measures instead of flattering

The check opens every page in a real browser at 390, 768 and 1440 pixels wide.

  • Hard errors: horizontal overflow, including from long German compound words, contrast to WCAG AA, tap targets that are too small, missing alt text.
  • Slop signals: 22 typical patterns, counted against a threshold instead of banned outright. One pill above a heading is not a mistake, six are a pattern.
  • Token drift: colors set outside your own system, measured with CIEDE2000.
  • Similarity: how close a new theme is to earlier ones, so that not every project looks the same.

That is why Nordlicht gets 92 instead of 100: six uppercase labels above headings against a threshold of two, plus a single italic word in the main heading. The check tells you what can be measured. Whether a draft is good is your call.

Your taste stays in house

For every reference, every theme and every direction you give a verdict: Love it, Good, Meh or No, plus one sentence on why. The sentence is stored word for word, not rephrased.

This builds a profile that Claude reads before any design work. Assessments made by agents are marked separately and do not count as your taste.

Clients get one link per moodboard. They see the three directions without a login and choose for themselves.

Built for agents

Kiekkist has a web interface for you and an MCP server for Claude with 17 tools: collect references, create boards, save directions, file themes, check, give verdicts. On top of that there is a REST API, a command line tool and an llms.txt.

The skill also works without the server. It then stores everything in the project folder under .kiekkist/, in the same format, and you import it later.

claude mcp add --transport http kiekkist \
  https://your-instance/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $KK_TOKEN"

Runs on your server

One Docker container, one SQLite file, one folder for screenshots. No third-party cloud, no subscription with a platform provider. The tool is licensed under AGPL-3.0, the skill under MIT.
